Project Description

“Rehabilitation Camp for Families of Fallen Heroes” is a 10-day program of psychological, emotional and physical support for mothers and children of Ukrainian servicemen who gave their lives for the country. The camp is held in the Carpathians and includes 10 mothers and 14 children aged 7–13.…
