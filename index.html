The idea

Simple enough to understand in one minute.

This website is designed around individual projects rather than long organizational explanations. Every project answers four questions: What is needed? Why does it matter? What will it cost? What happens when it is funded?

Large construction projects and small practical tasks can be displayed together because each is presented on its own merits, with photographs and a concise explanation.
