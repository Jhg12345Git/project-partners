Project Description

The available project inventory describes a summer rehabilitation initiative intended to provide psychological recovery for children and parents affected by war. The related inventory entry states a goal of conducting five camp sessions and serving more than 100 children and parents.

Purpose

Provide children and families with a period of safety, structured activities, psychological support, and recovery away from the continuing stresses of war.
